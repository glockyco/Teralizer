## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Cross-corpus generalization rates are not headline evidence

The producer SHALL NOT publish a controlled-versus-RepoReapers generalization-success comparison as a headline metric. Controlled RQ5 predates the widening license and lacks the telemetry needed to reconstruct a current-policy verdict. Attempt-level and filtering-retention rates MAY remain corpus-local diagnostic evidence.

A bounded RQ6 filtering comparison MAY place corpus-local retained shares together only when both observations count generalized tests with a filtering result, each share preserves its own denominator and provenance, and the result remains outside the thesis-wide headline key set. The producer SHALL NOT convert that comparison into one cross-condition effect or an overall applicability claim.

#### Scenario: Headline evidence is selected

- **WHEN** aggregate headline metrics are assembled
- **THEN** no metric pairs controlled `is_included` with a RepoReapers lifecycle state
- **AND** no metric presents 83.8%/30.2% or 84.0%/78.5% as a cross-condition effect
- **AND** the controlled and RepoReapers filtering-retention metrics are absent from the thesis-wide headline key set

#### Scenario: RQ6 receives bounded filtering evidence

- **WHEN** the producer publishes the controlled and RepoReapers filtering-retention observations
- **THEN** their metrics preserve separate corpus-local numerators, denominators, populations, and provenance
- **AND** the handoff permits only the interpretation that filtering retains a similar proportion in both settings
- **AND** final reader-facing wording and placement remain subject to the downstream thesis semantic review
